# Terms of Use: account, owner and support

## Definition

What the Terms of Use say about the account and its people: who owns an account and a store, the payment card, the administrator and moderators, ownership disputes, and how merchants reach CloudCart's support. Covers sections 5, 6, 10 and 21, with §8.10–§8.12, §8.19 and §8.21.

## Scope

The contractual rules only. The screens are on [[merchant]] (the account owner's record), [[settings-staff]] (administrators and moderators) and [[subscription-payment-methods]] (the card on file).

## Contrasts

- **Account vs store.** One account can hold several stores (§6.2). A store has exactly one owner (§6.1).
- **Owner vs administrator vs moderator.** The owner is the holder of the payment card (§5.8, §6.1). The administrator profile is created automatically for the person who registered the account, and there is only one (§6.3). Moderators get the rights the administrator gives them (§6.3).
- **Who may contact support vs who owns the account.** Administrators and moderators may write to support; nobody else can (§10.3, §21.2).

## Where it applies

### Registering (§5.1, §5.4, §5.5)

The merchant gives their name, company number (if any), address, a valid e-mail and any other information CloudCart requires. All of it can be edited in the admin afterwards (§5.1). CloudCart confirms the registration by e-mail (§5.4), and may refuse a registration that breaches the Terms or the law (§5.5).

### The owner is the card holder (§5.8, §6.1)

> „Онлайн магазин/Уеб сайт в платформата CloudCart може да има само един собственик. За собственик на Онлайн магазина/Уеб сайта се счита лицето, което е титуляр на платежната карта, която се използва за плащане на абонаментния план за магазина към КлаудКарт.“ (§6.1)

Everything done from the account, accepting the Terms included, is treated as done by the merchant and on their behalf. The merchant is fully responsible for it, authorised or not (§5.8).

### Several stores, one card (§6.2)

- One account can hold one or more stores, managed from **„Моите магазини“** in the admin.
- **Only one payment card** can be linked to an account, and every payment for every store on it uses that card.
- A merchant who wants to pay for different stores with different cards must create **separate accounts** for them.
- Each store may be invoiced to a different company ([[terms-of-use-billing-renewal]]).

### Administrator and moderators (§6.3, §6.4)

- The account has **one administrator**, created automatically for the person who registered it, and any number of moderators.
- The administrator gives moderators their rights, and each moderator may have different access levels.
- The merchant must mark in the admin whether the administrator is their employee or an outside person.
- The merchant answers for the administrator's and moderators' actions as for their own (§6.4).

### Ownership disputes (§8.10)

In a dispute over who owns an account, CloudCart may ask for any documents it needs, including:

- a certificate of good standing;
- an extract from the registers where the merchant is entered;
- an identity document;
- proof of ownership of the payment cards on the platform.

CloudCart then decides on the evidence, or deactivates the account until the parties agree.

### Transfer and resale (§22.3, §22.4, §8.19)

The merchant may not assign their rights (§22.4), but the Terms bind a successor who keeps using the platform (§22.3). Reselling CloudCart's services, or giving third parties access to them, needs CloudCart's express written consent (§8.19).

### Support (§10, §21)

- **Tickets only.** Technical support is given **only through the ticket system**. Each ticket has a priority and a response time, and the priority levels depend on the merchant's plan (§10.1).
- **Phone support** is offered on certain plans only. The pricing pages for each country say which (§10.2).
- **Who may write.** Only the merchant's administrators and moderators may open tickets or otherwise communicate with CloudCart. Messages from anyone else are not answered (§10.3, §21.2).
- **Channels.** The merchant may use the communication channels listed on CloudCart's website for their country (§21.1).

### Communications and publicity (§5.6, §8.11, §8.12, §8.21)

- Statements between the parties may be electronic, e-mail included. The e-mail and phone given at registration are the main channels, and CloudCart may also communicate through the platform's interface (§5.6).
- By accepting the Terms, the merchant agrees to receive promotional and marketing messages from CloudCart and its partners (§8.21).
- CloudCart may name merchants and show their logos on its own and its subsidiaries' websites, and use them in advertising, free of charge and without asking (§8.12).
- No merchant is promised exclusivity in a market segment (§8.11).
