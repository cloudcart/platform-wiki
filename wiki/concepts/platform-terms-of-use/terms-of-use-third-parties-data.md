# Terms of Use: third parties, services, IP and data

## Definition

What the Terms of Use say about the parties around the merchant:

- payment providers and couriers for the merchant's customers (§7, §12.9);
- third-party services in the platform (§14.1–§14.4, §14.11);
- the Experts marketplace (§14.5–§14.10);
- CloudCart's own additional services (§15);
- intellectual property (§13);
- data protection and confidentiality (§17, §9).

## Scope

Relations with parties other than CloudCart, and the paid services CloudCart offers on top of the platform. Fees for paying CloudCart itself, including bank fees on those payments (§11.12), are on [[terms-of-use-billing-renewal]].

## Contrasts

- **A payment provider of the merchant's choice vs CloudCart Pay.** For an integrated provider, the merchant contracts with the provider directly, and CloudCart is not a party (§7.2). CloudCart Pay is a separate service with its own agreement, and the general Terms do not mention it ([[cloudcart-pay-merchant-terms]]).
- **Expert vs CloudCart's additional service.** An Expert's service is a contract between the merchant and the Expert, paid through CloudCart (§14.6). An additional service is performed by CloudCart itself on an e-mailed offer (§15.1).

## Where it applies

### Payments and delivery for the merchant's customers (§7, §12.9)

- The merchant chooses which integrated payment methods their customers can use. A method that is not integrated may be added if it is technically possible and both sides agree; the merchant pays CloudCart extra for that integration (§7.1).
- The merchant registers their own accounts with the payment providers and connects them to the store (§7.2):

> „КлаудКарт не е страна по договора между Търговеца и доставчика на съответната разплащателна услуга и не носи отговорност пред Търговеца и неговите клиенти във връзка с предоставянето на разплащателната услуга.“ (§7.2)

- CloudCart calculates shipping prices through courier integrations but does not provide transport. The merchant contracts with a courier or arranges delivery in another way (§7.3).
- CloudCart does not sell the merchant's products. It is not responsible for their sale, payment, delivery, complaints or returns (§12.9).

### Third-party services and their fees (§14.1–§14.4, §14.11)

- CloudCart may give access to third-party software and services, such as payment, courier, ERP and warehouse systems and e-mail providers. It does so as a convenience; the third party provides the service (§14.1).
- Using them is at the merchant's discretion and risk (§14.2). CloudCart gives no warranty for them. They are governed by the third party's own terms and privacy policy, which the merchant is responsible for reading (§14.3).
- **Extra fees:**

> „Във връзка с използването на услуги на трети страни е възможно Търговецът да дължи допълнително плащане на КлаудКарт и/или на доставчиците на тези услуги.“ (§14.4)

- CloudCart may remove a third-party service from the platform at any time, but must then provide a similar or equivalent one (§14.11).

### The Experts marketplace (§14.5–§14.10)

- Experts are independent providers of web design, branding, marketing, advertising, accounting, legal, translation, photo, video and other services (§14.5).
- Pressing **„ПОРЪЧАЙ“** (or a similar button) concludes a contract **directly with the Expert**, on the terms in the Expert's profile. CloudCart is not a party and is not liable for the Expert's work (§14.6).
- Other terms may be agreed with the Expert, but not a different price. Extra or wider orders must also go through the platform (§14.7). The merchant's contact details are shared with the Expert (§14.8).
- Experts are paid **only through CloudCart**, in full and in advance, by the account's card (§14.9).
- **No refunds.** If the Expert did not deliver what was agreed, CloudCart provides another Expert at no extra cost. Subjective dissatisfaction is not a ground for this when the work matches the agreed terms (§14.10).

### CloudCart's additional services (§15)

Design, content, store setup, SEO and similar work (§3.6) is offered by e-mail, with the scope, price and deadline (§15.1). Once the merchant accepts the offer, CloudCart works with full independence, but within the requirements the merchant gave in the e-mailed questionnaire. Subjective objections are no ground for a refund when the work matches the accepted offer and the questionnaire (§15.2).

### Intellectual property (§13)

- The platform and all its content, including the CloudCart trademark, belong to CloudCart (§13.1).
- The merchant keeps the rights to their own materials and to their store's database (§13.2), and receives a limited licence to the platform that ends with the contract (§13.3).
- **The merchant may not remove or hide information about CloudCart's intellectual-property rights in their store (§13.5).**
- CloudCart may remove infringing content (§13.7), and in an IP dispute may suspend services or deactivate the account until it is resolved (§13.9).

### Data protection and confidentiality (§17, §9)

- Personal data is collected and processed under **CloudCart's Privacy Policy**, published on CloudCart's websites (§17.1), and protected under the Bulgarian Personal Data Protection Act and the GDPR (§17.2).
- For unregistered visitors CloudCart records the IP address, browser, operating system, pages visited, and the date and time (§17.3).
- The merchant agrees that CloudCart may store information and access what is stored in the merchant's admin, for the purposes and periods set out in the Privacy Policy (§17.4). The merchant may object as the Privacy Policy describes (§17.5).
- Both sides keep each other's confidential information (§9). What the merchant publishes in the store is not confidential (§9.2).
- The Terms say nothing about using merchant or shopper data to train AI models. That would be a matter for the Privacy Policy.
