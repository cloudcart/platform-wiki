# Headless — legacy-vs-Nitrogen surface map

## Definition

A Nitrogen storefront answers on two kinds of address, and who answers a path depends on which:

- **A store domain routed to the storefront** (see [[headless-nova-platform]]): the platform keeps a fixed list of paths. Everything else goes to the storefront's worker.
- **The storefront's own `{slug}.cloudcart.dev` address**: nothing is reserved. Its worker answers every path, those on the list included.

This page maps what a merchant inherits on a routed domain and what the storefront has to do itself.

## Scope

Covered:

- The paths the platform keeps on a routed domain.
- `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, the sitemap and the feeds.
- 301 redirects, e-mail links, languages and the private store.

Not covered:

- General SEO mechanics — see [[seo-handling]].
- The CloudCart admin, which is the same whichever storefront serves the domain.

## Contrasts

- **Platform paths vs storefront paths** — on a routed domain the platform answers the list below. The storefront answers `/`, products, collections, search, cart, account, pages, blogs and policies.
- **Routed domain vs `cloudcart.dev` address** — the robots.txt a crawler gets on a routed domain is the merchant's from the admin. On the `cloudcart.dev` address it is whatever the storefront's worker returns.

## Where it applies

### Paths the platform keeps on a routed domain

The admin's confirmation says: *"These keep working on the same domain: the admin panel (/admin), product images, checkout and payments, the sitemap, the feeds, robots.txt and llms.txt."* In full:

- **Exact paths:** `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, `/feed`, `/google-adwords.csv`, `/cc-sw.js`, `/firebase-config.js`, `/firebase-messaging-sw.js`, `/OneSignalSDKWorker.js`, `/OneSignalSDKUpdaterWorker.js`, `/OneSignalSDKUpdateWorker.js`.
- **Path prefixes:** `/admin`, `/api/sf`, `/api/gql`, `/api/v2`, `/cdn/`, `/checkout`, `/payment`, `/restore-abandoned`, `/checkout-link`, `/embed`, `/bbt`, `/pixel`, `/creditor`, `/crossSell`, `/up-sell`, `/.well-known/`, `/sitemap`, `/xml_feed`, `/csv_feed`, `/json_feed`, `/app/feed/`, `/download/` (a product file's link).

A storefront route under one of these never runs on a routed domain.

### `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, sitemap and feeds

On a routed domain these are the platform's:

- `/robots.txt` serves the merchant's text from [[marketing-seo-robots]], or the default, or the `Disallow: /` block on a trial or expired store (see [[seo-robots-served-file]]).
- `/llms.txt` serves the merchant's text or the generated one, and answers 404 on a trial or expired store (see [[marketing-seo-llms]]).
- `/sitemap*` and the product feeds are generated by the platform, as for any store.

On the `cloudcart.dev` address the storefront's worker answers these paths, if it has routes for them. `Shop.llmsTxt` gives a storefront the text to serve there. Its schema text says a headless storefront always serves it itself, which holds only on that address.

### 301 redirects

The rules on [[marketing-seo-301-redirects]] stay readable after a cutover, but the storefront has to apply them, since old addresses reach its worker. Before answering "not found", it asks `urlRedirects(query: "path:/old-address")`, without a trailing slash. The answer holds the one redirect the classic storefront follows for that address: entered as the address itself, with a trailing slash, or as a `*` pattern that covers it. Without `path:`, the field lists every rule, oldest first. Both are empty when the store has redirects turned off.

### Cart, payment and order pages

- **Cart widget:** the storefront draws its own cart from `/api/sf`. The classic theme's cart bubble is not loaded.
- **Payment:** `/checkout` and `/payment` stay on the platform on a routed domain, so provider returns and webhooks keep working. `Cart.checkoutUrl` opens the web checkout on the store's main host (see [[headless-checkout-submit]]).
- **E-mail links:** the confirmation warns: *"Links in the store's e-mails that open the platform's pages, such as unsubscribing or an order's page, reach the storefront on this domain and do not work there yet."*

### Languages

A store serves one language: the Storefront API accepts `@inContext(language:)` and `@inContext(country:)` and ignores them, and it does not read `Accept-Language`. A storefront cannot get another language by asking the API.

Each language is its own store, with its own storefront and domain (see [[apps-multilang]] and [[multi-language]]). Routing such as `/en/` and `/de/` inside one storefront changes only the storefront's own texts, not the catalogue's.

### Private store

The classic storefront sends a guest from its home, catalog, product, search and blog pages to the login page when the Private store app requires registration. Through the API the storefront decides: `Shop.requiresCustomerLogin` says the store is closed to guests, and the storefront should open on sign-in and registration. See [[headless-catalogue-reads]] and [[apps-private-store]].

### Quick reference

| Surface | On a routed domain | On the `cloudcart.dev` address |
|---|---|---|
| `/robots.txt`, `Disallow: /` on trial or expiry | Platform | The storefront's worker |
| `/llms.txt` | Platform | The storefront's worker (`Shop.llmsTxt`) |
| `/sitemap*`, feeds | Platform | The storefront's worker |
| Web checkout (`Cart.checkoutUrl`) | The store's main host | The store's main host |
| Payment returns, images | Platform | The store's own hosts |
| 301 redirects | The storefront, with `urlRedirects` | The storefront, with `urlRedirects` |
| Pages opened from store e-mails | Reach the storefront | The e-mail links point at the store's domain |
| Other languages | Another store and storefront | Another store and storefront |
| Private store | The storefront, with `Shop.requiresCustomerLogin` | The same |
