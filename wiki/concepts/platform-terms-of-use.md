# CloudCart Terms of Use (hub)

## Definition

The **Общи условия на платформа за онлайн магазини CloudCart** are the general contract between CloudCart and every merchant. A merchant accepts them when registering an account: filling in the registration form, ticking the consent box and pressing **„Напред“** (§1.2). Acceptance concludes a binding contract (§1.3).

- **Where:** https://my.cloudcart.com/bg/legal/terms-of-use (https://cloudcart.com/bg/terms-of-use redirects there). All earlier versions are also published on CloudCart's websites (§18.6).
- **Version:** adopted on 09.04.2023, last amended on 27.11.2024 (the document's closing line).
- **Language:** the Terms are written in Bulgarian. If a translation differs, the Bulgarian version applies (§22.5).
- **Summaries are not the contract.** The **„ГОРНОТО ОЗНАЧАВА“** boxes and the section titles are there for convenience only. They have no legal force and do not interpret the Terms (§1.3, §22.6). Quote the numbered clause, never the summary.

The full text is kept unchanged as a raw source: `raw/sources/2026-11-09--cloudcart-terms-of-use-bg.md`.

### Who may sign up (§5.2)

Only businesses. The merchant must act within their trade or profession, registered in the Commercial Register, BULSTAT or another trade register where applicable, or act on behalf of a trader. On accepting the Terms the merchant declares that they are **not a consumer** under Art. 2(1) of Directive 2011/83/EU.

### Law and disputes (§20, §22)

Disputes are settled by negotiation first, then by the competent Bulgarian court (§20.1–20.2). Bulgarian and EU law apply, and the UN Convention on the International Sale of Goods is excluded (§22.1). If one clause is invalid, the rest stays valid (§22.2). The Terms bind the merchant's successors (§22.3). The merchant may not assign their rights (§22.4).

## Scope

Covers the general Terms of Use, Bulgarian version, as amended on 27.11.2024.

Does not cover:

- **CloudCart Pay**, which has its own agreement and fee schedule: see [[cloudcart-pay-merchant-terms]] and [[cloudcart-pay-pricing]]. The general Terms do not mention it.
- **CloudCart's Privacy Policy** (§17) and **Anti-spam policy** (§8.20). The Terms refer to them but do not reproduce them, and they are not ingested here.
- The **additional conditions for specific services** published on CloudCart's corporate website next to plans and services (§1.1).
- The merchant's **own** storefront terms and privacy policy for their customers: see [[apps-gdpr-policy]].

## Contrasts

- **The contract and the platform's behaviour.** The Terms say what CloudCart may do or commits to. Feature pages say what the platform currently does. Where current practice is more generous than the contract, support may describe the practice but must not present it as a contractual right. For example, data kept beyond the 30 days in §11.11 and §19.3: see [[terms-of-use-termination-data]].
- **General Terms and the CloudCart Pay agreement.** Changes to the general Terms need 15 days' notice (§18.1). The CloudCart Pay agreement uses 30 days, and its English text prevails ([[cloudcart-pay-merchant-terms]]).
- **Clause and summary.** A „ГОРНОТО ОЗНАЧАВА“ box is a plain-language summary without legal force (§22.6).
