--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The topics a new store starts with (Knowledge & Skills → Conversation topics):

product_recommendation   Препоръка на продукт        Helping them choose what to buy.
product_information      Информация за продукт       A question about a specific product: specifications, materials, what fits what.
availability             Наличност                   Whether something is in stock, or where it can be seen.
order_status             Статус на поръчка           An order already placed: where it is, when it arrives.
delivery                 Доставка                    How delivery works: prices, deadlines, couriers, areas.
returns                  Връщания и рекламации       Returning, exchanging, or complaining about something bought.
stores                   Магазини и работно време    The shops themselves: addresses, opening hours, contacts.
other                    Друго                       Anything that fits none of the others. (Cannot be removed.)

Conversations not yet filed are shown on the dashboard as "Некласифицирани" (unfiled).
