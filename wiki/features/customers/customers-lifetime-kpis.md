# Customers — Lifetime KPIs + deletion cascade

## Purpose

The denormalised lifetime totals that the customer list and detail pages read **straight from the customer record** (not from a live aggregation over orders). This aspect covers which fields are denormalised, when the recompute job runs, the EUR conversion rule, the CloudCart-staff-only backfill commands, the 1-hour password-reset link validity, AND the full deletion cascade (cart deleted, Subscriber deleted async, past orders preserved, default address nulled, `isEmpty` protection).

## Where to find it

- Customer list ([[customers-list-view]]) → **Completed orders** + **Revenue** columns read from the denormalised totals.
- Customer detail insights module on [[customers-details]] → lifetime KPI summary.
- Backfill commands → CloudCart-staff-only artisan, not in the admin UI.

## What the merchant can do here

- See up-to-date lifetime totals on every customer in the list — driven by the queued recompute job.
- Recognise stale totals and contact CloudCart support to request a backfill (see Business rules below).
- Bulk-delete customers (with `isEmpty` protection on non-empty records) — see [[customers-bulk-actions]].

## Settings & fields

### Denormalised lifetime fields — verified

Stored ON the customer record:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `income` | Lifetime customer revenue (store currency). |
| `completed_orders` | Count of completed orders. |
| `orders_total` | Count of all orders the customer placed (regardless of status). |
| `orders_total_price` | Sum of all order totals (regardless of status). |
| `last_order_date` | Timestamp of the most recent order. |

These are written by the queued the platform code whenever an order is created, paid, refunded, cancelled, fulfilled, etc. — triggered by the order lifecycle.

### Backfill commands (CloudCart-staff-only)

CloudCart staff can rebuild the totals via artisan:

- `php artisan customer:populate-income {--site=<id>} {--from=<customer_id>}` — bulk-rebuild ALL customers on a site (resumes from a customer ID if interrupted). Use after a migration / data-fix that touched orders.
- `php artisan customer:update-income {--site=<id>}` — refresh totals for customers with stale flags (lighter / iterative variant).
- `php artisan customer:validate-income {--site=<id>}` — read-only check that compares stored totals against a live re-computation; reports mismatches without writing.

Merchants cannot trigger these from the admin UI — they must open a support ticket.

### Password-reset link validity — verified

The reset-password code generated by the bulk action ([[customers-bulk-actions]]) and the "forgotten password" flow lives for **3600 seconds** (1 hour) after creation. After that the link is invalid and the customer must request a new one. The validity timestamp is included in the success toast message: *"A link to reset your password has been sent and is valid until [datetime]"*.

## Business rules

### Income / KPIs are denormalised + async

The customer's lifetime KPIs are STORED on the customer record itself — not computed live from orders on every page load. The queued the platform code recalculates them whenever an order is created, paid, refunded, cancelled, fulfilled, etc. — triggered by the order lifecycle.

**Practical implication**: after an order's status changes (e.g., a refund just processed), the lifetime KPIs on the customer list and detail page MAY be a few seconds behind reality. They converge once the job runs. For "income doesn't match the sum of orders" tickets, CloudCart staff run the backfill commands above.

### EUR-store conversion at 1.95583 BGN/EUR

When the store currency is EUR, BGN-denominated orders are converted at the fixed rate **1.95583 BGN/EUR** before adding to the customer's total.

### `isEmpty` protects against accidental deletion — verified

The customer model has an `isEmpty` check (true when the customer has NO shipping addresses, NO billing addresses, AND NO orders). The delete flow is expected to refuse deletion of customers who fail this check — protecting historical order data. The merchant should manually archive non-empty customers rather than deleting. (verify the merchant-UX response when trying to delete a non-empty customer via the modern UI — error toast vs silent block.)

### Deletion side effects (cascade) — verified

When a customer is deleted (single or bulk via [[customers-bulk-actions]]):

- Their **cart is deleted** (a model "deleting" hook removes any active Cart record for that customer).
- The **`customer.deleted` webhook** fires (per [[settings-hooks]]).
- Their **Subscriber record is deleted asynchronously** via a queued job (`OnCustomerDeleted` on the `subscribers` queue) — removes the customer from any marketing segments, newsletter lists, and campaign channels keyed by their email/phone/messenger identifiers.
- Past **orders are NOT deleted** — they remain with their snapshot customer data (no foreign-key cascade configured). The `default_address_id` foreign key uses **`ON DELETE SET NULL`**, so the address-default is also automatically cleared when the address is removed.
- **Hard delete only** — there is no soft-delete on the Customer model, so deletes cannot be recovered from the UI.

### Guest → registered auto-merge inherits KPIs

When the merchant adds a customer via [[customers-create-modal]] with an email that matches a guest record, the platform promotes the guest to a registered customer — and the lifetime KPIs (income, completed_orders, orders_total) on the "new" registered customer immediately reflect the prior guest orders, since the customer entity is the same row. No separate KPI recompute is needed — the totals were already on that record.

### Permission

Reading the KPIs requires `customers` API permission ([[settings-staff]]). The backfill commands are CloudCart-staff-only (artisan, not exposed to merchants).

### Side effects pipeline (summary)

| Trigger | Async job | Effect on KPIs |
|---|---|---|
| Order created / paid / refunded / cancelled / fulfilled | the platform code | Recomputes the five denormalised fields. |
| Customer deleted | `OnCustomerDeleted` on `subscribers` queue | Removes Subscriber row. |
| Marketing flag flipped | `OnMarketingChange` | Updates Subscriber's marketing flag (see [[customers-flags]]). |
| Tag added / removed | `CustomerTagChange` propagation | Updates Subscriber tags. |
| Inactive → active activation | Welcome Email (+ Confirmation Link Email when `unconfirmed_accounts_restrict != 'none'`) | See [[customers-flags]]. |
