# Subscription transactions — invoices

## Purpose

The **invoice side** of a subscription's transaction history: how the merchant downloads the invoice PDF for an Approved charge, the rules that govern that PDF (language, site-scoping, best-effort generation), download access during account suspension, and the actions the merchant **cannot** take from this surface (resend, edit, export, refund). The on-screen table is on [[subscriptions-transactions-table]]; the record fields on [[subscriptions-transactions-fields]].

## Where to find it

[[subscriptions]] → click a row → [[subscriptions-detail]] at `/admin/details/subscriptions/<unique_id>` → the transactions table below the info cards. Each **Approved** row has a **Download** button in the **Actions** column (and in the condensed expandable row on [[subscriptions]]).

## What the merchant can do here

### Invoice download

The **Download** button on each Approved transaction opens the invoice PDF in a new tab. The PDF is generated server-side from the invoice template — see [[details-billing]] for the merchant's invoicing settings that drive the PDF layout (recipient info, numbering, language). The endpoint is `admin.subscriptions.invoice` on the legacy stack and `/admin/api/core/invoice/download/<invoice_id>` on the modern stack.

Only **Approved** transactions have a Download button — declined / voided / refunded rows produce no invoice, so there is nothing to download (see [[subscriptions-transactions-fields]] for why).

### What the merchant CANNOT do here

- **Resend an invoice email** from this screen. Invoice emails are sent automatically at renewal time — there's no "Resend invoice" button in this surface.
- **Edit a transaction** (amount, description, card used). All transaction rows are immutable historical records.
- **Refund a transaction** from this surface. Refunds for plan / app / feature subscriptions go through customer support, not the merchant's self-service.
- **Re-attempt a declined transaction.** The retry happens automatically on the platform's backoff schedule (see [[subscriptions]] → Renewal retry schedule). The merchant can trigger an immediate fresh charge by clicking **Renew** on the list page — that fires a new charge attempt, not a retry of the specific declined transaction.
- **Export** the transactions to CSV / Excel. No export action.
- **Edit the invoice PDF** before downloading. The PDF is generated from the merchant's stored invoicing settings + the transaction data at the moment of issuance.

## Settings & fields

Two fields drive the Download action: `invoice_id` (set only when `approved == 1`; points to the issued invoice) and `invoice_number` (the issued invoice's number, shown in the **Document number** column). Both are null on declined attempts. The full per-field table is on [[subscriptions-transactions-fields]].

## Business rules

### Approved transactions get an invoice; declined don't

Only Approved transactions are paired with an invoice (`invoice_id` set, `invoice_number` populated, Download button visible). Declined transactions are recorded for the audit trail but never produce an invoice — there's nothing to download.

### Invoice number ≠ transaction reference ID

The **Document number** (invoice number) is generated by the merchant's invoicing settings sequence and is the legal accounting reference. The **ID** (transaction `reference_id`) is the payment processor's reference, used by support to investigate gateway-side issues. They serve different purposes — both are surfaced in different columns of [[subscriptions-transactions-table]].

### Invoice PDF language follows the recipient's language

When the invoice was issued, the platform stored the recipient's language preference. The PDF is rendered in that language regardless of the merchant's current admin-panel locale. So a Bulgarian admin downloading an EUR-region invoice will get the invoice rendered in the recipient's language (e.g., English / Greek / Macedonian) — not in Bulgarian.

The download endpoint also loads additional view + translation paths from the console area before rendering, so the invoice PDF can use console-specific templates / strings that aren't normally available in the merchant admin's view stack. This explains why invoice PDFs may use slightly different wording than the merchant's admin UI.

### Invoice download is scoped to the merchant's site

The download endpoint validates that the requested invoice belongs to the current site before serving the PDF. The merchant cannot download another store's invoices by guessing the invoice ID.

### Invoice PDF generation is best-effort

The invoice-download endpoint wraps PDF generation in a try/catch — if generation fails (e.g., template error, missing data), it returns an empty 200 response rather than an error page. The merchant sees an empty PDF download rather than an error message. (This is a known behavioural quirk — when troubleshooting an empty download, the merchant should contact support.)

### Invoice download works even when admin access is restricted

The invoice download URL validates site ownership but does NOT gate on the merchant's suspension state. So a suspended merchant who STILL has the invoice URL (e.g. saved from an earlier email) CAN download historical invoices via that URL. In practice they're typically locked out of the admin panel UI altogether (which is where the Download button lives), so they need to keep the email-delivered link to retrieve invoices during suspension.
