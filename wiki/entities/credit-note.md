# Credit Note

## Identity

A **Credit Note** is the formal accounting document the merchant issues against an existing [[invoice|Invoice]] to document a refund or cancellation — the tax-document counterpart to the invoice, required by tax law in most jurisdictions whenever a sale is reversed. Each Credit Note is generated by the platform's active Invoicing provider (CloudCart's built-in renderer, or an external ERP such as Szamlazz configured on [[settings-invoicing]]) and is downloaded as a PDF. It prints the **issuer block**, **customer block**, **line items being credited**, **totals** (including VAT reversal), **parent invoice reference**, and a fixed **number + date** pair. Once a Credit Note number is consumed, it can never be reassigned — Credit Notes are part of the merchant's permanent tax audit trail.

There are **two kinds**, drawn from one shared number series:

- a **whole-order** Credit Note, issued from the order's **View credit note** dropdown once the order is cancelled / refunded. Its number and date live on the [[order|Order]] as `credit_number` / `credit_date`. At most one per Order.
- a **partial** Credit Note, issued against a **return** for part of the order. Its number and date live on the return record as `credit_note_number` / `credit_note_date`. An Order can accumulate several of these. See [[orders-returns-lifecycle]].

An Invoice is therefore **not** limited to one Credit Note — but the sum of all non-cancelled Credit Notes on an Order can never exceed the Order total.

A Credit Note is distinct from an **Invoice** ([[invoice]]): an Invoice records a sale, a Credit Note records its reversal. It is also distinct from a **payment refund** ([[orders-payment-refund]]): the payment refund is the money movement at the payment provider; the Credit Note is the accounting document proving the refund happened. The two often pair but are independent actions.

## Aliases

- **Credit Note** — the canonical merchant-facing term in the admin UI ([[orders-credit]] action title), sidebar, and customer emails.
- **Credit memo** — accounting jargon for the same document; less common in the UI.
- **Refund document** — informal merchant phrasing emphasising the refund-proof nature.
- **Order credit note** — used when distinguishing from other document types.
- **Кредитно известие** / **Кредит нота** — Bulgarian terms; "Кредитно известие" is the standard accounting label.
- Credit Note numbers in the admin, emails, and downloaded PDF use the merchant's configured format, built from the three separate `credit_number_formatting_prefix` / `_padding` / `_suffix` settings on [[settings-invoicing]] (e.g., `CRN-2026-000045`) — see [[credit-note-numbering]]. There is no single pattern field.

## Key Attributes

The six aspect pages above own the substantive detail. The storage shape in one paragraph:

A **whole-order** Credit Note has no record of its own — it is the rendered PDF plus the `credit_number` + `credit_date` fields on the parent Order. A **partial** Credit Note lives on the **return** record that produced it (`credit_note_number` + `credit_note_date`), which is why an Order can carry more than one. Either way, re-downloading always re-renders fresh from the current Order data, with only the number / date frozen — see [[credit-note-template-rendering]].
